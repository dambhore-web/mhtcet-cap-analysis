The estimate is a range, so it's drawn as a band on the familiar barcode of every branch cutoff: the blue lines inside the band are branches that may or may not be within reach. The buttons explain which end to plan with instead of making the student pick a number blind.

Percentile or merit number?

Your percentile compares you with everyone in your exam session. Your state merit number is your rank in the CAP merit list, which CET Cell publishes after registration. CAP allots seats by merit number, so Compass uses it for every result.
