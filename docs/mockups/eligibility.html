Which seats can you apply for?

Besides general seats, CAP keeps seats for specific groups. Tick the ones that apply to you and Compass adds them to your results.

Each card shows what it's worth to this student before they tick it: the extra branches that seat type opened for merit 12,000 last year, from the real search. Cards for seats the student can't claim (EWS for an OBC student) stay visible but greyed, with the reason.

Home university (H, O)
Some seats are kept for students who passed Class 12 in the same university area. Add your home university in My details and Compass uses it.
Ladies seats (L…)
Reserved for female candidates. Included automatically when your details say Female.
Category seats
OBC, SEBC, SC, ST, VJ/DT, NT-B, NT-C, NT-D. Used when your category is set. You need a valid caste certificate and, where required, a validity certificate.

Rules and certificate formats are set in the official CAP information brochure; the CET Cell's document check decides. Read the seat-code guide · Ask Compass
