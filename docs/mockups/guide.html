How CAP works

  1. Register and verify documents

    Fill the CAP application on the CET Cell portal and get your documents verified (e-scrutiny or at a facilitation centre).

  2. Check the merit lists

    A provisional merit list comes first, then the final state merit list. Your state merit number is your rank in it.

  3. Fill the option form

    List up to 300 choice codes (college + branch) in the order you prefer them. You're allotted the highest choice your rank qualifies for. Build yours

  4. Round I allotment

    CET Cell publishes the allotment. If you got a seat, you choose to freeze, float or slide, and report online or at the institute.

  5. Rounds II and III

    Seats left after each round are allotted again. You can edit your option form between rounds. Later rounds usually close at higher (easier) ranks.

  6. Report to the institute

    Once you freeze a seat, report to the college with original documents and pay the fees before the deadline.

Freeze, float or slide

What happens to your seat in the next round. The arrow shows where you can still move.

Freeze

Accept this seat and stop. You won't be considered for higher choices again.

Float

Keep this seat and stay in line for higher choices at any college. If one opens you move up; if not, you keep this seat.

Slide

Keep this seat and stay in line only for higher choices at the same college. You never move to another college.

The auto-freeze rule overrides float and slide: a seat from your top choices locks (Round I: choice 1, Round II: 1–3, Round III: 1–6, as published for 2025-26).

Seat codes

Every closing rank belongs to a seat code. Type one from a cutoff list to see what it means.

The seat-code table becomes a decoder: the code splits into its three parts as you type, which teaches the pattern (quota, category, level) instead of a 60-row glossary.

FAQ

Can I change my choices after Round I?

Yes, between rounds the option form opens again and you can edit it, unless your seat was frozen. Check each round's dates on the CET Cell portal.

What is TFWS?

Tuition fee waiver seats: the college waives tuition for Maharashtra candidates within the family income limit in that year's brochure. Other fees still apply. See if it applies to you

Is my percentile the same as my merit number?

No. Your percentile compares you with your exam session; your merit number is your rank in the CAP merit list. Estimate it

Will this year's cutoffs be the same?

No one knows yet. Compass shows last year's official closing ranks as a guide; this year's can move.

Dates and rules change every year. Always follow the official CAP information brochure and CET Cell notices.
