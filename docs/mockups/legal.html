Disclaimer

Unofficial product

Compass is an independent information tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Maharashtra State Common Entrance Test Cell (CET Cell), the Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), the Government of Maharashtra, or any university listed on this platform.

Past data, not a guarantee

All cutoff data on Compass comes from official DTE Maharashtra / CET Cell publications for the 2026 academic year. It reflects past admission rounds only and does not predict or guarantee any future outcome. Cutoffs in upcoming rounds may differ significantly.

Use at your own discretion

Compass helps students and families understand their options. It is not a substitute for official DTE communications, allotment letters, or counselling. Always check admission details at cetcell.mahacet.org before acting.

Accuracy and completeness

We work to publish accurate, complete data but can't guarantee it is free from errors, omissions or delays. If you find a discrepancy, please report it.

Fee data

Annual fees are based on Fee Regulating Authority (FRA) orders and are indicative. Confirm fees directly with the college.

Privacy

Your details and option form are saved in this browser only. Compass never stores candidate names or application IDs from the official lists.
