## Findings from the staging data (2026-09-27), needed for step 1
- **Round semantics:** each round's official value covers only the seats allotted **in that round**. Seat types without new allotments are absent, and values can go down between rounds. The "later round" status uses the highest value published in Rounds II–IV (`eligibility-rules.md` §6).
- **Sections (MH list):** `State Level` 49,302 · `Home University Seats Allotted to Home University Candidates` 30,225 · `Other Than Home University Seats Allotted to Other Than Home University Candidates` 19,076 · `Home University Seats Allotted to Other Than Home University Candidates` 3,258 · `Other Than Home University Seats Allotted to Home University Candidates` 2,965 · `Minority Seats Allotted to Maharashtra State Candidature Candidates` 218 · `Maharashtra State Seats Allotted to All India Candidature Candidates` 9. The candidate's HU status picks the relevant sections (the HU→OHU cross sections are leftover seats).
- **Stages:** `I` 84,984 · `VII` 9,973 · `I-Non PWD` 4,638 · `I-Non Defence` 3,164 · `II` 1,635 · `MH` 659. 828 (choice, round, section, seat type) combinations have several stages, e.g. PWDOPENS stage `I` 191,334 vs `I-Non PWD` 39,955. Proposal: match on stage `I` by default. Stage `II` = ladies seats given to male candidates (brochure rule 10 Stage II), so it's relevant to male candidates on `L…` seats. Meanings of `VII`, `I-Non PWD`, `I-Non Defence` and `MH` are `UNKNOWN`: confirm from the "Cut Off Circular" notice on fe2026.mahacet.org before using them.
- **College Home University** (`college.home_university`): `Autonomous Institute` 131 (State Level seats only, no HU split), then university names (Savitribai Phule Pune University 83, Mumbai University 47, …), null 3. The candidate's HU dropdown should use these names.
- **College status** (from the institute list): Un-Aided 206, Un-Aided Autonomous 96, minority variants carry the community (e.g. `Religious Minority - Muslim`).
- **Percentile input:** the state merit list isn't loaded yet. Interim option: estimate CET percentile → state merit from the (closing_merit, closing_percentile) pairs in MH cutoff rows, labelled "estimated". Better: have AG-002 parse `FE2026_PCMMH_MeritList_Final.pdf` (64 MB, listed in `docs/02-architecture/data-sources.md`).
